# Solo — Designer Rulings

These rulings come from the game's owner after reviewing `readings.md`. Where they differ from `readings.md`, **these win**. Use them together with the rulebook and the spreadsheet.

## Rules

All 20 rulebook interpretations in section 1 of `readings.md` are **confirmed as written**.

## Cards

Everything in `readings.md` stands, including its [UNCLEAR] assumptions, except the changes marked below.

**Confirmed (your reading was right):**

- **"Play" from discard or deck** means the card's combo-enter ability triggers: Ember, Blood Cross, Forest Hook, Death Roundhouse.
- **Phoenix Finisher:** "this turn" means this round.
- **Life Roundhouse:** must choose *another* card. It can't return itself.
- **Ink Chop, Ink Uppercut, Ink Roundhouse:** sabotage cards. They affect whichever combo they sit in, and are meant to be planted in the opponent's combo.

**Changed:**

- **Lion Finisher:** costs 1 less for each card **you** discarded this round. The opponent's discards don't count.
- **Sun Cross:** a **reaction** feint, like Flash Jab. When you discard another card, you may discard Sun Cross from your combo. If you do, return that card to your hand. It doesn't use your action.
- **Forest Oak:** the played Green card must be **exactly** 3 higher than the opponent's last card, not 3 or more.
- **Check Finisher** (added 2026-10-01): choose **one** face-up card in either combo and flip it 4 times: it turns face down once and its "when flipped" ability triggers 4 times. A reaction that stops the flip (Flash Jab, Life Jab) or Umbra Roundhouse stops all four. If you pick an opponent's card, its ability triggers for them.
